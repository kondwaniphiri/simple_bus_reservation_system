About Hard Work Logistics

Our service links the Copperbelt cities of Kitwe, Ndola and Chingola with Lusaka. With branches in these centres, we support local commuters, business travellers and passengers visiting family.

Visit a branch for travel information, boarding arrangements and help with your reservation. Once you create an account, you can check listed departures, choose a seat and keep your tickets in one place.

Where we travel

Kitwe

Our Copperbelt base connects passengers to nearby towns and longer journeys to Lusaka.

Kitwe Central Bus Station, Freedom Avenue

Ndola

Connections within the Copperbelt and onward travel between the region and the capital.

Ndola Bus Terminal, Broadway

Chingola

Serving the western Copperbelt with links to Kitwe, Ndola and Lusaka.

Chingola Bus Station, Independence Way

Lusaka

Our capital-city branch links Inter-City travellers with destinations across the Copperbelt.

Inter-City Bus Terminal, Lumumba Road

Travel questions

How much luggage can I bring?

Confirm luggage limits and any oversized items with your departure branch before travelling.

Can I cancel my booking?

Cancel through My bookings before the scheduled departure. Discuss any payment or refund arrangements with the branch.

How do I pay?

Your online reservation holds a seat. Confirm the accepted payment method and pay at the branch.

What do I need for boarding?

Bring your reservation number or printed ticket and arrive 30 minutes early. Confirm any identification or assistance requirements with your branch.
