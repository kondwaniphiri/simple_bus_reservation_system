About Us

With a strong presence across Zambia's key urban centers, our bus service proudly connects the Copperbelt heartland of Kitwe, Ndola, and Chingola with the nation's vibrant capital, Lusaka. From our operational bases in each of these strategic locations, we facilitate seamless travel for commuters, business professionals, and leisure seekers alike. Our deep understanding of the transportation needs within and between these significant cities allows us to offer reliable, comfortable, and punctual journeys. Whether you're traveling within the Copperbelt's interconnected network or embarking on the longer route to or from Lusaka, our dedicated team and well-maintained fleet ensure a smooth and stress-free travel experience across these vital Zambian destinations.

Locations

Our Reach: Connecting Zambia's Key Cities We've established a vibrant network across Zambia's most dynamic urban centers, ensuring seamless travel between the Copperbelt – encompassing Kitwe, Ndola, and Chingola – and the bustling capital, Lusaka.
Kitwe: Rooted in the heart of the Copperbelt, our Kitwe hub is more than just a location; it's our foundation. We provide reliable and comfortable connections within the region and act as a vital link to broader destinations.
Ndola: In the welcoming city of Ndola, a crucial transport nexus, our presence facilitates effortless onward journeys. We're here to connect you smoothly within the Copperbelt and beyond.
Chingola: Serving the western Copperbelt, our Chingola location ensures that this important mining community and its surrounding areas have access to dependable transportation solutions.
Lusaka: Our strategic hub in the energetic capital city provides a vital artery connecting the Copperbelt with the center of Zambia. We offer frequent and comfortable services, bridging distances for business, education, and personal connections.
Our commitment to these four key locations – Kitwe, Ndola, Chingola, and Lusaka – underpins our mission to provide a stylish, clean, and dependable travel experience that keeps Zambia connected.
